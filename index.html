Part I.A — Problem Analysis (Manual vs Centralized Monitoring)

Below are 8 core operational problems identified in traditional manual or fragmented scholarship-monitoring processes:

No. Problem Identified Root Cause Systemic Effect / Impact
1 Delayed Deficiency Identification Manual grade review using physical paper transcripts and spreadsheet passing Scholars receive grant stipends for semesters after losing eligibility, causing financial overpayments.
2 Fragmented Scholar Profiles Scholarship data stored in disparate Excel spreadsheets across multiple departments Data redundancy, conflicting scholar records, and missing historical academic compliance records.
3 Inconsistent Rules Enforcement Varying scholarship conditions (e.g. Merit vs Needs-based GWA cutoffs) manually checked Human evaluation errors, unfair grant renewals, and student grievances due to subjective decisions.
4 Unverified Grade Submissions Lack of verification step before approving grade submissions Tampered or unverified self-reported grades used for final scholarship compliance evaluation.
5 Lack of Auditability & Traceability No centralized change logging or history of record modifications Inability to track who approved or altered a grade record or overridden compliance status.
6 High Staff Workload & Bottlenecks Manual computation of GWA and manual verification of unit loads during peak enrollment Processing backlogs, delayed clearance for enrollment, and administrative exhaustion.
7 Poor Visibility for Stakeholders No self-service portal for scholars to view compliance status or pending deficiencies Excessive student inquiry phone calls/emails and missed deadlines for grade submission.
8 Inaccurate Management Reporting Manual aggregation of scholarship statistics for committee meetings and donors Inaccurate reporting on total active scholars, retention rates, and fund distribution.

Part I.C — Requirements Elicitation (12 Structured Interview Questions)

  1. Current Workflow: What exact step-by-step procedures are currently followed from scholar registration to end-of-semester evaluation?
  2. Grade Submission: How do scholars currently submit their semester grades, and what supporting proof (e.g. TCG, portal screenshot) is required?
  3. Deadline Enforcement: What happens when a scholar fails to submit grades within the prescribed submission deadline?
  4. Scholarship Criteria: How do academic requirements (GWA threshold, minimum units, failing grade rules) differ between Merit, University, and Private grants?
  5. Grade Verification: What specific verification checks must staff perform before a grade submission is marked as authentic?
  6. Deficiency Handling: What constitutes a deficiency, and how are conditional/probationary statuses granted versus immediate disqualification?
  7. Renewal Process: What criteria and approval workflow govern the transition from "For Renewal" to "Renewed" for the upcoming academic year?
  8. Notification & Alerts: How are scholars notified regarding pending submissions, deficiency warnings, or status changes?
  9. Reporting Requirements: What specific summaries or compliance breakdown reports are required by executive management and scholarship sponsors?
  10. Access Control: Who should have authorization to modify verified grades, override compliance decisions, or configure program criteria?
  11. Auditability: What details must be logged when an authorized user modifies a verified grade or overrides a scholar's compliance status?
  12. System Integration: How should the system integrate with existing user authentication (Supabase Auth / Student ID accounts)?

BR-03A grade submission must belong to one scholar, academic year, and semester.

BR-10Sensitive grade information must only be visible to authorized users.

Detailed Use Case 1: Submit Grades (UC-04)

Primary Actor: Scholar / Scholarship Staff

Goal: Submit academic semester grades and unit loads for verification.

Preconditions: User logged in; Scholar record exists and assigned to active program.

Trigger: Scholar opens grade submission interface at end of semester.

Main Flow:

  1. User selects scholar profile, academic year, and semester.
  2. User inputs GWA, units enrolled, count of failing subjects, and incomplete subjects.
  3. System validates input ranges (GWA > 0, non-negative counts).
  4. System saves submission record with status set to Pending / For Verification.
  5. System updates scholar status to For Verification.

Alternative Flow: Staff submits grades on behalf of scholar during walk-in verification.

Exception Flow: Invalid GWA format or blank fields block form submission with error warning.

Postconditions: Submission created in database; notification badge updated.

Detailed Use Case 2: Verify Grade Submission (UC-06)

Primary Actor: Scholarship Staff

Goal: Verify authenticity of submitted grades against official transcript data.

Preconditions: Submission exists in Pending status; user authenticated as Staff.

Trigger: Staff clicks "Verify Submission" on pending queue.

Main Flow:

  1. Staff reviews submitted GWA, unit load, and subject counts.
  2. Staff checks official registrar grade release.
  3. Staff clicks "Confirm & Verify".
  4. System records verified_by user ID and current timestamp verified_at.
  5. System updates submission status to Verified.
  6. System triggers automated compliance evaluation engine.

Alternative Flow: Staff detects discrepancy and marks submission as Returned with remarks.

Postconditions: Verified record is locked for final compliance evaluation.

Detailed Use Case 3: Evaluate Academic Compliance (UC-07)

Primary Actor: Scholarship Coordinator / Automated System Engine

Goal: Compare verified semester metrics against assigned scholarship program criteria.

Preconditions: Grade submission is Verified.

Trigger: Completion of verification or manual execution of compliance batch run.

Main Flow:

  1. Engine retrieves program rule parameters: required_gwa, min_units, allow_failing_grade.
  2. Engine evaluates: GWA ≤ required_gwa AND units ≥ min_units AND fails == 0 (if policy disallows).
  3. IF all conditions hold, system tags evaluation as COMPLIANT and updates scholar status to Compliant.
  4. IF any condition fails, system tags evaluation as WITH DEFICIENCY and logs specific deficiency causes.

Exception Flow: If scholar has no verified submission, evaluation is skipped.

Detailed Use Case 4: Process Scholarship Renewal (UC-12)

Primary Actor: Scholarship Coordinator

Goal: Review compliant scholars at end of academic year for grant renewal.

Preconditions: Scholar has status Compliant or For Renewal.

Trigger: Coordinator initiates annual renewal processing batch.

Main Flow:

  1. System filters scholars with status For Renewal or Compliant across both semesters.
  2. Coordinator selects eligible scholars and clicks "Approve Renewal".
  3. System updates scholar status to Renewed and increments active academic year.
